FamilyBookkeeping — Support

Help for the FamilyBookkeeping mobile app (iOS & Android).

Common topics

  • Getting started: Sign in, create or join your family chain (when connected to your backend), then add expenses, miles, and hours.
  • Offline use: Your data can be stored on your device. Optional hybrid sync sends changes to your configured backend when online.
  • Backend / sync issues: In the app, open Settings → confirm the backend URL, hybrid mode, and run the backend connection test if available.
  • Subscriptions: Purchases are handled through Apple App Store or Google Play; manage or cancel in your store account settings.

Contact

For app support, please email your-support@yourdomain.com — update this address in public/support.html before you submit to the stores.
